# Nobel 2026, Medicine: the light switch (2:21, 16:9; the same story as a 9:16 cut and as an interactive page that stops and asks)

Contract: In about two minutes a curious non-scientist follows a "far-fetched" idea from an alga to this year's Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, makes three guesses along the way, tries our toy model of how far light reaches and how much it heats, and ends knowing that the limit today is how deep light can go into the brain.

Rules: every factual sentence is a CLAIMS.md row (ids in the last column; "the Committee says" wherever the row is the committee's statement); every toy-model number comes from the simulator results (ids R1-R3, filled in after the review fixes) and is always called "our toy model"; the three colours in the toy model are all caveated the same way; showtime is credited only on the end card. Roadmap bar (always visible after shot 2): "1 The alga / 2 The switch / 3 A toy model / 4 The limit".
