# Claims ledger: Literature 2026 (Anne Carson)

> Educational demos made to show an open-source tool. Not research.

Every factual sentence we say in the video or on the page, or print in a README of this folder, comes from this
table. Wording may shorten a row, never extend it. If a sentence changes, its row is checked again. The wider
background is in [FACTS.md](FACTS.md).

**Check date:** 2026-10-08. The Nobel pages were read from copies saved that morning and the Nobel API was pulled
live; the ancient-author sources were fetched by `survival-ledger/ledger/check_sources.py`, which found all 46 stored
quotes and the key words of the 7 paraphrased entries (6 Suda On Line, 1 World History Encyclopedia) on their pages ([source_check.json](survival-ledger/results/source_check.json)).

**How verified**

- **Nobel text**: the Swedish Academy's press release (English and Swedish), the nobelprize.org summary and facts
  pages, the list of women laureates, and the Nobel Committee's bio-bibliography (signed Anders Olsson).
- **Nobel API**: the Nobel Prize API v2.1 (CC0), counted by `forms/shelf/analysis.py`; the snapshot is committed.
- **ancient testimony**: the Suda (Suda On Line translations, cited by entry and said in our words: their licence,
  CC BY-NC-SA, does not fit this MIT repository) and other ancient witnesses as cited.
- **edition / scholarship**: Grenfell and Hunt 1914, reviews and surveys (BMCR, LRB, Cambridge, Durham), Wikipedia for
  facts only. Each with its URL and quoted words (paraphrase for the Suda) in [survival-ledger/ledger/rows.py](survival-ledger/ledger/rows.py).
- **arithmetic / our count**: a number we computed from a sourced fact. The steps are in the row.

**Status:** "verified" means the sentence is supported by the source as it stood on the check date. "rule" rows set
how something may be used. "will not say" rows exist to stop a sentence. "pending" rows belong to another lane and are
not to be used until that lane marks them verified.

## The Committee quote rule (L09)

Anne Carson's own words appear in this folder only as short quotations: the few listed under "Quotations" below
(L59, L60, L62 for the page and the READMEs; for the video, L60 again and L64, L65; L61 is withdrawn), and one line the Nobel Committee itself quotes:
"hold in equipoise two perspectives at once", attributed "Anne Carson, *Eros the Bittersweet* (1986), as quoted by the
Nobel Committee". At most once per artefact (each README, the page, the video). Never together with the Committee's
other quoted phrase from the same book (the two-word one in the same sentence). This ledger and FACTS.md are
ledgers, not artefacts: they hold the Committee's whole sentence as evidence. Not spoken over, not animated letter by letter as if
written by us, never translated or paraphrased.
