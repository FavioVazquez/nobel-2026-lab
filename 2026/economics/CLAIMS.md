# Claims ledger: Economic Sciences 2026 (Robert E. Hall, Michael Woodford)

> Educational demos made to show an open-source tool. Not research, and not economic or policy advice.

Every factual sentence we say in the film or on the page, or print in a README of this folder, about the prize, the
laureates and their work, or about our toys' numbers, comes from this table. Wording may shorten a row, never extend
it. If a sentence changes, its row is checked again. The wider background and the source keys in square brackets are
in [FACTS.md](FACTS.md).

**Check date:** 2026-10-12 for every row. The nobelprize.org documents were read from copies saved on the day of the
announcement; the PDFs were read page by page; the Nobel API was pulled live and counted by us. The toy numbers
(sections 20 and 21) were checked against the toys' results JSON on the same day, field by field, by a script.

**How verified**

- **Nobel text**: the press release (PR), the popular information (POP), the scientific background (SB) and the
  summary page (SUM), and the "Prize in Economic Sciences" page (ECO). SB pages are PDF pages (the number a PDF viewer
  shows); the printed number is the PDF page minus 2.
- **Nobel API**: the Nobel Prize API v2.1 (CC0), counted by us; the counts are also stated on nobelprize.org.
- **Nobel API terms**: the terms of use for the API and data.
- **toy JSON**: a number computed by one of our toys; the row names the JSON field path and its value.
- **editorial rule**: how something may be used; no outside source.

**Status.** "OK" means a sentence may be said in our own plain words as written. "OK-quote-only" means only as a
quotation in quotation marks, credited, never restated in our voice. "DO NOT USE" means not used on screen, on the
page or in a README in any voice; the reason follows. "charged" rows are committee sentences that read as advocacy
(section 18). Every status carries the check date.

**Quote rule.** Fewer than 15 words per quotation. Credit: PR and POP = "Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences"; SB = "the
prize committee's scientific background". A longer sentence is split into quoted parts, never paraphrased into our
voice. We add no comment of our own to a committee line about policy.

**Voice rule.** Model results are said as the model's results ("in the model, ..."), never as advice. We take no side
on stabilisation policy, central banks or any school of economics.

## Discrepancies found in the sources

1. Page numbers: the scientific background's printed page number is the PDF page minus 2; this ledger uses PDF pages only.
2. Inflation-shock start year: SB PDF p.49 "beginning in 2021" vs PDF p.50 "began in 2025" (E158).
3. Equation order: SB PDF p.10 calls the price equation "the third"; pp.7 and 30 call it the second (E075).
4. Output gap: POP says Woodford "introduced" the concept; the SB says he defined natural output in a new way and made the gap the key variable (E098, E100).
5. 80/20: POP speaks of 80 % of households; the SB of 80 % of consumption (E055, E056).
6. Forward-guidance sentence on SB PDF p.40 reads inverted (E118).
7. SB reference Woodford (1996b) has a title that does not match "the first version of the New Keynesian model" (E173); the 1988a/1988b working-paper remark differs between SB PDF p.18 and p.23 (E174).
8. Mishkin's first name: POP "Fredric", SB reference "F. S." (E053).
