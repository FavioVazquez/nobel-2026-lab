      The lab with three equations

      Spend

      Households look ahead: they spend on what they expect, and a higher real interest rate makes them spend later.

      Price

      Firms that set prices look ahead, because changing a price is costly: expected inflation and today’s output gap move inflation.

      Rate

      A rule sets the interest rate from inflation and the output gap.
